# Captions, original commentary and localisation

## Captions

1. Transcribe only audio the application is authorised to process.
2. Separate speech recognition from speaker attribution; do not invent speaker identities.
3. Preserve source timestamps and map them through the edit decision list after trimming, speed changes or montage edits.
4. Generate concise, readable captions with correct spelling, punctuation and football terminology.
5. Validate every caption segment against audio/video; detect overlap, missing words, implausible timing and text outside safe zones.
6. Support user-configured languages. Translations must preserve meaning and must not add unsupported match facts.
7. Keep captions distinct from platform-generated subtitles. Burned-in captions are part of the rendered file; uploaded subtitle tracks are a separate optional feature and must use supported API capabilities.

## Original editorial contribution

For a reused sports clip, create meaningful original value such as tactical explanation, context, analysis, critique or a clearly authored story. A title, music bed, crop, subtitles, emojis or a simple montage alone are not a reliable substitute for substantive commentary.

The system must not fabricate match facts, player names, scores, quotes or tactical claims. Ground statements in verified metadata and observable footage. Flag uncertainty. Use original scripts and licensed or user-authorised voice assets.

## Voice and synthetic audio

Use only voice recordings or synthetic voice services for which the user has appropriate rights and consent. Do not imitate a real commentator, player or public figure without the necessary permission. Keep voiceover provenance and generation settings.

## Editorial safety

Check for graphic injury, violence, slurs, abusive chants, personal information, minors, betting promotions and other sensitive or advertiser-suitability risks. Escalate uncertain cases for human review; do not automatically publish risky material.

## Acceptance tests

Caption timestamps align after edits; spelling and language checks run; no unsupported facts are generated; every commentary track has provenance; minimal edits without meaningful contribution are flagged; unsafe segments block or require review.
